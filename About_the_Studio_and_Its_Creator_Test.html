About the Studio and Its Creator

Where the words began

Words That Needed Out Studio began with words I didn't always know where to put, feelings I had carried for a long time, and stories that needed to be heard. I discovered that putting those feelings into words—and turning those words into songs—gave me a way to express things I couldn't always say out loud.

What began as one song led to another, and then another. It became a little like finding a tangled ball of feelings and gently pulling one string at a time. Each song helped bring something to the surface: a memory, a hurt, a hope, or a piece of a story that had been waiting to come out. Creating became a form of therapy for me—a way to work through what I had carried inside for years and turn it into something meaningful.

Why I created this studio

I wanted to make a place where other people could do something meaningful with their own words, memories, and music. Whether you're creating a memorial for someone you love, a birthday surprise, an anniversary gift, or a video for another special moment, the process should feel welcoming—not confusing or overwhelming.

My goal is to make the studio as easy to use as possible, with a clear step-by-step format. I also believe people should know the cost before they choose to create. No unpleasant surprises at the end: the goal is to show the full price upfront so you can decide what works for you and your budget. I want the tools to be affordable and the experience to feel personal, creative, and accessible.

This studio grew out of my own need to let the words out. I hope it gives someone else a way to tell their story, honor a memory, celebrate a person they love, or create something they can keep and share.

Tragedy to Triumph. Real life. Real feelings. Real songs.
